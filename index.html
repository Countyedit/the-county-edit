What Is It

One place for local life.

The County Edit is a beautifully curated guide to the very best of local life in Rutland and Stamford, trusted businesses, local events, seasonal guides and everything worth knowing about life here.

Think of it as your modern local guide, thoughtfully designed to help people discover the very best of county life.

Why It Exists

We noticed a familiar problem.

Like many people, we found ourselves constantly asking:

Can anyone recommend a good builder?

What's happening this weekend?

Who did you use for that?

Where's good for a special dinner?

At the same time, so many brilliant local businesses, events and independent brands were scattered across social media, magazines, Facebook groups and word of mouth, often impossible to discover unless you already knew where to look.

The County Edit was created to change that.

What Makes It Different

Curated, not crowdsourced.

Unlike open directories or Facebook recommendation groups, every business on The County Edit is reviewed before it appears. We care about quality, trust and presentation, because the businesses here deserve to be shown at their best, and the people discovering them deserve to trust what they find.

For those who remember reaching for the Golden Pages whenever they needed a trusted local business, The County Edit is our modern take on that idea, thoughtfully curated for the way we discover local life today.

Our Commitment

Standards that matter.

We carefully review every business before they appear on The County Edit. This isn't about gatekeeping, it's about creating a platform people can genuinely trust. When you find a business here, you can feel confident it's been chosen with care.

Testimonials are verified. Profiles are professionally presented. And we're always here if something isn't right.
